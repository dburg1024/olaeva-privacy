# OLAEVA Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 24, 2026**

OLAEVA is developed and published by **Derrick Burgan Jr.** This Privacy Policy explains what information OLAEVA accesses, how that information is used, what is stored on your device, and when information may be provided to other services.

## Information OLAEVA Accesses

### Location

If you allow location access, OLAEVA uses your location while you use the app to provide nearby places, Explore and Discover features, weather, route planning, local context, and other location-based functionality.

OLAEVA may also use compass heading and direction information for features such as Discover.

You can choose whether iOS provides OLAEVA with precise or approximate location and can change or revoke location access at any time in iPhone Settings.

### Calendar

If you grant Calendar access, OLAEVA reads upcoming event information such as event title, time, and location so it can provide Plan Ahead information and estimate when you may need to leave.

OLAEVA does not create, edit, or delete your calendar events.

You can deny or revoke Calendar access at any time in iPhone Settings.

### Information You Save or Enter

OLAEVA may store information you choose to provide or create within the app, including:

- your preferred name and app preferences;
- favorite, saved, and recent places;
- search history;
- planned routes and stops;
- current trips;
- geographic reminders and related details.

This information is stored locally on your device. OLAEVA does not currently provide an OLAEVA account system or first-party cloud storage service.

## Local Storage and Notifications

OLAEVA uses on-device storage to remember preferences and features you choose to save.

A short-lived Today cache may include local-news article information, a locality name, location coordinates, and the time the information was loaded.

If you choose to schedule alerts, OLAEVA uses Apple's local notification system. Notifications may contain information such as an event or place name, reminder text, or an estimated leave time.

OLAEVA does not operate a remote push-notification server.

You can remove saved information using available controls within OLAEVA, including clearing supported history, canceling trips or tasks, removing saved items, and canceling alerts. Location, Calendar, and notification permissions can also be managed through iPhone Settings.

## Apple Services

OLAEVA uses Apple technologies and services to provide certain features.

### Apple Maps and MapKit

OLAEVA uses Apple MapKit and Apple Maps for place searches, nearby results, route estimates, geographic information, and navigation that you choose to open.

When you use these features, Apple may receive information necessary to provide the requested service, such as search terms, destinations, transportation mode, or your location when location access is authorized.

Apple's handling of information through these services is governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

### Apple WeatherKit

OLAEVA uses Apple WeatherKit to request weather information for a location.

Weather information and related requests are processed through Apple's WeatherKit service and are subject to Apple's privacy practices.

## GDELT Local News

OLAEVA may use your location to derive a city, county, or regional description and send that locality information to the GDELT API in order to provide relevant local news and context.

OLAEVA does **not** send your precise geographic coordinates to GDELT as part of this local-news query.

GDELT is an independent external service. Its handling and retention of request, access, or network information is governed by its own practices. OLAEVA does not represent that GDELT deletes or does not retain request information.

## Links and Services You Open

Certain OLAEVA features may allow you to open an external search, website, map, or other third-party destination.

For example, if you choose an external search shortcut, the name of a place or destination may be provided to that service.

Once you leave OLAEVA or choose to use another service, that service's own privacy practices apply.

## Voice Guide

OLAEVA's optional Voice Guide uses Apple's speech-synthesis technology to provide spoken walkthrough guidance.

OLAEVA does not record your voice and does not send voice recordings to an OLAEVA server.

You may use OLAEVA without enabling the Voice Guide.

## Accounts, Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

OLAEVA currently does not provide an OLAEVA user account system or first-party backend service.

OLAEVA does not currently use:

- advertising SDKs;
- third-party analytics SDKs;
- Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA);
- cross-app tracking;
- data-broker tracking;
- first-party behavioral advertising systems.

OLAEVA does not sell your personal information.

## Children

OLAEVA is a **general-audience application** and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

OLAEVA does not provide an OLAEVA account system through which it knowingly collects personal information from children.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of location, Calendar, external websites, and other device features as appropriate.

## Your Choices and Controls

You can control many of OLAEVA's data-related features directly through the app or through iPhone Settings.

Depending on the feature, you may be able to:

- deny or revoke location access;
- use approximate rather than precise location;
- deny or revoke Calendar access;
- deny or revoke notification permission;
- remove saved places or history;
- cancel planned trips or reminders;
- disable the Voice Guide.

Some OLAEVA features may not function fully when the permissions they depend on are disabled.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when OLAEVA's features, services, or data practices change.

When the policy is updated, the Effective Date at the top of this page will also be updated.
