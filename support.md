## Common Help

### Location

OLAEVA uses location to power nearby places, Explore, Discover, weather, route planning, and other location-based features.

You can review or change location access in:

**iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → OLAEVA**

### Calendar

Calendar access is optional and is used for Plan Ahead features such as upcoming events, travel estimates, and leave-time reminders.

You can review or change Calendar access in:

**iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → OLAEVA**

### Notifications

Notifications are optional and are used for local reminders such as Plan Ahead alerts.

You can review or change notification access in:

**iPhone Settings → Notifications → OLAEVA**

### Voice Guide

The optional Voice Guide is available during the first-use walkthrough and can be turned on or off there.
